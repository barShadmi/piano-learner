# Phase 1 · Task 3 · Sampled grand piano in memory, velocity layers, pedal

Goal: the P-121 sounds like a real grand piano through the PC. The stand-in triangle voice from task 2 stays only as the sound you hear for the second or two while the piano loads.

Decisions (lead):
- Samples: Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm, CC BY 3.0, recorded at 48 kHz stereo, every minor third from A0 to C8 (30 notes), 16 velocity layers. We ship 4 layers (Salamander layers 4, 8, 12 and 16) as OGG Vorbis, about 20 MB, copied from the npm packages `@audio-samples/piano-velocity4|8|12|16@1.0.5` into `assets/piano/` and committed (they are data, not code; no npm dependency). Credit in `assets/piano/CREDITS.md` and one quiet line on the Sound screen: "Piano sound: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0)."
- Memory: decoded at start-up on a background thread (lewton 0.10.2) into i16 stereo, at most 10 s per sample with a 1 s fade at the cut (piano tones are nearly silent by then). About 180 MB of RAM. The app starts with the stand-in voice and switches to the piano when loading finishes (usually 1 to 3 s).
- Playback: each key uses the nearest sampled note (at most ±1.5 semitones away) resampled by `2^(semitones/12) × sampleRate/outputRate` with 4-point cubic (Hermite) interpolation. Velocity picks a layer (1-40 → L4, 41-72 → L8, 73-100 → L12, 101-127 → L16) and scales gain within the layer with the existing velocity curve (normalised so the layer boundaries do not jump). Crossfading layers is not needed for phase 1.
- Key up: 120 ms damper fade. Keys above MIDI 88 (E6) have no dampers on a real grand, so they ring on after key up (until their sample ends or 10 s). Sustain pedal holds as in task 2. Same-note repress fades every older voice of that note (including ones in release) over 5 ms (fixes the task 2 note).
- Headroom: the note mix goes through a master gain of 0.5 and a soft limiter (`x / (1 + |x|)`-style knee above 0.7, never above 1.0) so a loud pedalled chord never hard-clips. Tested.
- Handoff to the audio thread: the loaded `SampleBank` is shared as `Arc<SampleBank>`. A new output start builds its `EngineCore` with the bank (on the starting thread). If an output is already running when loading finishes, the Arc goes in through a small rtrb ring of `Arc<SampleBank>`; the audio thread swaps it in. Anything the audio thread would drop goes back through a return ring and is dropped on the control thread, so the audio thread never frees memory.

## Step 1 · Sample bank and piano voice (builder-plus, hard: true)
Files: `assets/piano/` (OGG files named `<Note><Octave>v<layer>.ogg`, e.g. `C4v8.ogg`, `D#4v16.ogg`, plus `CREDITS.md`), `native/audio-engine/Cargo.toml` (lewton), new `src/piano/mod.rs`, `src/piano/bank.rs` (file names → MIDI note + layer, loading, trimming, fade), `src/piano/voice.rs` (sample voice, cubic resampling, damper, no-damper top keys), `src/piano/limiter.rs`, `src/core/mixer.rs` (note voices play from the bank when one is set, else the stand-in voice), `src/core/mod.rs` (bank in/out rings), `tests/no_alloc.rs`.
Tests first: file-name parsing (A0 → 21, C8 → 108, D#4 → 63, F#7 → 102); nearest sample for every key 21..108 is at most 2 semitones (1.5 rounded) away; a synthetic bank (a generated sine "sample" at a known pitch) played 1 and 2 semitones up gives the right frequency (zero crossings ±1%); layer choice at velocities 1, 40, 41, 72, 73, 100, 101, 127; damper fade within 150 ms; key 96 keeps sounding after key up (no pedal); limiter: output never above 1.0 for 64 loud voices, and below 0.7 it changes nothing; loading the real `assets/piano` folder gives 120 samples, all 48 kHz stereo, each at most 10 s, last sample 0, and takes under 5 s in release mode (print the time); no allocation in `process` with the bank set and when the bank arrives through the ring.
Acceptance: cargo test, clippy (plain and all-targets), Windows check pass; a small `examples/render_scale.rs` writes a WAV of a C major scale played at three loudnesses to the scratchpad, and the verifier listens by looking at the waveform peaks (soft < medium < loud) and checks no sample is above 1.0.

## Step 2 · Loading in the app (builder-plus: it touches the engine's bank handoff)
Notes from step 1: the bank goes in with `Mixer::set_bank` before `EngineCore::with_midi` for a new start, and through `piano::bank_link` (`BankSender`/`BankReceiver`, `EngineCore::with_bank_link`) when sound is already running. `Engine` owns the `BankSender` and calls `reclaim()` on start, stop and status. Only one bank is ever sent (a second swap mid-play would click). CI runs `cargo test --release` once so the load-time check runs.
Files: `src/api.rs` (`loadPiano(folder): Promise<{ samples, seconds }>`, `pianoStatus(): { state: 'loading' | 'ready' | 'failed', error? }`), `src/output/mod.rs` (engine keeps the bank and hands it to every start), `index.d.ts`, `src/main/audio.ts` / `audioController.ts` (load from `assets/piano` in development and `process.resourcesPath/piano` when packaged, right after sound starts), `electron-builder.yml` (`extraResources` for `assets/piano`), `src/main/ipc.ts`, `src/preload/*` (`audio.state()` gains `piano: 'loading' | 'ready' | 'failed'`), `src/renderer/src/screens/Sound.tsx` + `soundText.ts` (line "Piano sound is loading…" / "Piano sound is ready." with the credit line), `e2e/app.spec.ts` (the Sound screen shows the piano line; the piano loads in CI without a sound card), CI check that `resources/piano/*.ogg` (120 files) is in the Windows build.
Acceptance: npm test, lint, build, e2e pass; screenshots light and dark; CI green.
